# Format Playbook — Quote Tweet

last_updated: 2026-06-12
status: hand-seeded (01-spec.md §4.5)

A quote tweet amplifies the original to the **persona's own followers**. Different reach mechanics from a reply: it spends the persona's own credibility on someone else's content, and it's read by an audience that may never open the quoted tweet.

## Rules

- **Must stand alone.** A reader who never opens the quoted tweet should still get the point. If the quote text only makes sense alongside the original, it should be a `reply` instead.
- Quote when the persona has a take that's **bigger than the thread** — something worth surfacing to their own audience, not just a response to the original author.
- Don't quote when the point only makes sense inside the conversation (that's `reply.md` or `thread-reply.md`'s job).

## When a quote beats a reply

- The persona has an independent observation that the quoted tweet is a good illustration of, but the observation itself is the point — the quoted tweet is supporting evidence, not the subject.
- The persona wants to amplify something genuinely good to their own followers (amplify+extend archetype, `reply-playbook.md`, applied as a quote instead of a reply).

## When a quote is worse than a reply

- The point is really "I agree, and here's the missing piece" addressed to the original author/thread — that reads better as a reply, in place, where the original author and thread participants will see it.
