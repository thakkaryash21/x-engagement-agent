# Anti-AI Writing Guide Template

Use this as the final pass before any draft enters the queue.

## Avoid

- Generic openers
- Formulaic contrasts
- Inflated claims
- Forced lists of three
- Polished language that the persona would never use

## Prefer

- Specific claims grounded in the company facts file
- Natural sentence variation
- The persona's observed phrasing and pacing
- Direct statements over performative structure
